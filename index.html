Making images that stay with you

I'm a photographer based in Edmonton, AB. My work moves through automotive, portrait, lifestyle, and events which are held together not by subject but by a sustained interest in what light does to a surface, and what a surface does to a viewer.

"Photography is the art of observation. It has little to do with the things you see and everything to do with the way you see them."

This archive is a record of that practice. Not a catalogue of services — a body of work that accumulates meaning over time. If something here resonates, I'd be glad to work together.

The Practice

Four disciplines. Each approached on its own terms, with full attention.

01

Portrait

Made in collaboration — searching for the moment when the mask slips and something true surfaces.

02

Machines as sculpture. Geometry, drama, patience — treating the car as a subject worthy of a portrait.

03

Events

Catching the in-between — the laugh before the speech, the quiet edge of the dance floor.

04
